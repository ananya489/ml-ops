Why we need MLFlow ?
-Imagine you train the same model or the same datset ten times , changing the model parameters , dataset version
-without experiment tracking we might end up with file names model_v1.joblib,model_v2.joblib and so on..
-MLFlow helps us to maintain that record

4 major concepts:
--Experiment :Collection of related training  runs 
--Run :One execution of training with specific parameters and results
--Artifact: Saved file produced by a run
--Model registry: Managed of registry models and their versions
